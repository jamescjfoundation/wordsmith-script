# Code of conduct

This project is made by volunteers for the people who use it. We want everyone
who takes part, in issues, pull requests, reviews and discussions, to be
treated with respect.

## Expected

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers and people writing in a
  second language.
- Criticise work, not people. Explain what you would change and why.
- Accept that maintainers decide what goes in, and say why when they say no.
- Respect people's privacy: do not post anyone's personal information, and do
  not share writing or files that were not meant for the public.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults, slurs, threats, or sexual language or imagery.
- Attacks on someone for who they are: their age, body, disability, ethnicity,
  gender identity and expression, experience, nationality, appearance, race,
  religion, or sexual identity and orientation.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Repeatedly disrupting discussions after being asked to stop.

## Reporting

Report a problem privately to the maintainers: open the **Security** tab of
this repository, choose **Report a vulnerability**, and start the title with
"Conduct:". Only the maintainers can read it. Say what happened, where, and who
was involved, with links where you can.

Maintainers will review every report, keep the reporter's identity private,
and may edit or remove comments, lock threads, or block people from the
project. A maintainer involved in a report takes no part in deciding it.

## Scope

This applies in this repository and anywhere someone represents the project.
