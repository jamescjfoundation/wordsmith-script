# Roadmap

v0.1 covers milestones M1 to M8 in the kickoff plan. These are deliberately
out of scope for v0.1:

- Proprietary file formats, including `.fdx`. Interchange in v0.1 is Fountain,
  our native format, PDF, RTF and plain text. A proprietary format needs a
  legitimately published description first.
- Real-time collaboration.
- Cloud sync.
- AI features. Hooks may exist behind a flag that is off by default.
- Production breakdown tagging and scheduling export.

Later candidates from the spec, unordered: teleplay and stage-play templates,
structure colors, writing stats and sprint timer, dual dialogue, inclusivity
report, story board images and PDF export.
