# Editing tools

- **Return and Tab hint.** The status bar names the element at the caret and
  what Return and Tab make from it.
- **Reset to Element** (Mod+Shift+R) drops formatting set by hand. Notes,
  revision marks, locks and bookmarks stay.
- **Toggle Case** (Mod+Shift+K) puts the selection in capitals, or back in
  small letters.
- **Smart Quotes** and **Capitalize Sentences** (Format menu) are off until
  you turn them on for a script. Common abbreviations such as "Mr." do not
  start a new sentence.
- **Select Scene** (Mod+Shift+A), **New Scene** (Mod+Shift+N) and **Page
  Break** (Mod+Shift+Return). A page break at the start of a paragraph that
  already has one removes it.
- **Structure elements.** Sequence and the outline elements are under Format ›
  Structure and Outline, and on Mod+Shift+1 to 7.
- **Go To** (Mod+G): a page (a number, or a label such as 12A when pages are
  locked), a scene number, or a bookmark.
- **Bookmarks** (Mod+M adds one at the caret; Mod+Shift+M lists them). Tick
  "Label" to make it a label. A bookmark stays with its paragraph as you edit.
- **Find** can be limited to one element.
- **Reformat** (Tools menu) steps through paragraphs: press a digit to make
  the paragraph that element and move on, D to delete it, the arrows to move.
- **Check Format** (Tools menu) finds paragraphs that start with spaces, empty
  paragraphs, names with no speech, the same speaker twice in a row, and
  dialogue with no name. The first three can be fixed in one click. You can
  have the check offered or run before printing.
