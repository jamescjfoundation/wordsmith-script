# Outlining

The story board and the outline show the same beats, so a change in one is a
change in the other. Beats are saved with the script.

## Story board

**View › Story Board** (Mod+Alt+6) is a canvas of colored cards. Double-click
the canvas to add a beat there, drag beats to move them (they snap to the
grid unless you switch it off), and drag from the dot on a beat's right edge
to another beat to draw a link; links stay attached as beats move. Select a
beat to color it, edit it, delete it, or **Send to Script**: its title goes
into the script at the caret as an Outline 1 paragraph and its body as a
Summary. In the beat window, **Tab** moves between the title and the body.

## Outline

**View › Outline** (Mod+Alt+7) shows lanes, one per row: acts, sequences or
anything you like. Add lanes yourself or from a structure (three acts, five
acts, eight sequences, a television episode), rename them in place, move and
indent them. Drag beats between lanes and along them. Below the lanes are
the beats in no lane, and the Script lane: your scenes with the page each
starts on. A beat sent to the script shows the pages it covers.

## Outline elements in the script

Outline 1 to 3, Summary and Note paragraphs are for you, not the reader.
**View › Show Outline Elements** switches them off in the page view, the PDF
and printing; they stay in the script and come back when you switch it on.
