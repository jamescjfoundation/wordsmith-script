# Characters, scenes and reports

## Scene properties

Script › Scene Properties shows the scene at the caret: its heading, a title, a
color, the summary, and who is in it. Speakers are listed automatically; you
can hide one from the scene's cast, add someone who is there but does not
speak, and write an arc beat for each character. All of it moves with the scene
when you reorder scenes.

## Characters

Script › Character Details holds a character's traits (Age, Gender, Role and
the rest; add your own) and a highlight color. Turn the highlight on and every
speech of that character is tinted on screen (never in print). Renaming a
character carries all of this to the new name.

## Tables

Click a column's name to sort by it (again to reverse, a third time to go back
to script order). Type in Filter to keep matching rows. Columns hides or shows
columns. The + button keeps the table as it is now as a tab of your own, which
you can rename, save or remove; your tabs are saved with the script. Trait
columns and the highlight switch are in the Characters table; notes have a
title and a type.

## Reports

Tools › Reports (Mod+Shift+J): scenes, locations, cast, one character, the
filtered script, notes, statistics and the representation chart. Each can be
sorted where it makes sense and saved as text or CSV; the filtered script can
also be saved as a PDF laid out as a script.

- **Scenes:** number, heading, title, page and length; the longest and shortest
  scene; the last page. Production order groups scenes by location.
- **Locations:** each place with its scenes, intros and times of day; headings
  missing a part are listed.
- **Cast:** speeches, words, speaking and non-speaking scenes; who speaks most
  and least.
- **One character:** the scenes they are in (with arc beats), who they share
  scenes with, their monologues (100 words or more), and optionally every
  speech.
- **Statistics:** words and paragraphs per element, per character and per
  scene, other scenes at the same place, and the flagged words with their pages
  and speakers.
- **Representation:** characters grouped by two traits, with their share of the
  characters, the dialogue and the scenes.
