# Boards, cards and the structure strip

## Story boards

A script can have several boards: the tabs above the board pick one, + adds
one, and Rename and Delete Board change the one showing. Beats you add go on
the board showing.

Shift-click beats to select several, then use **Arrange** to put them on the
grid, in a column, in a row, or in a cascade. With a beat selected, **Links**
lists its flow lines: draw each as a plain line, an arrow either way or both
ways, pick its color, or remove it.

In the beat window, "• List" and "1. List" start a new list line in the body.

**Export PDF…** prints the board showing on one landscape page.

## Index cards

Choose how many cards to a row (or Fit), and whether cards show the summary or
the start of the scene's text. A card shows the scene's title and color, and
its Properties button opens Scene Properties.

**Print Cards…** saves the cards as a PDF: one per 3 × 5 in card, or a grid on
Letter pages with your choice of columns and rows, frames and a grey
background.

## Structure strip

View › Structure Strip (Mod+Alt+S) shows a band above the script: a lane for
Outline 1, acts and sequences, and one for scenes, each block as long as the
pages it covers, with page numbers along the top. Click a block to go there.
The slider zooms the strip from 25 % to 400 %. Agents and the command palette
can choose which elements feed the lanes (`strip.configure`).

Outline paragraphs shown in the script have colored bars in the left margin,
one per level.
