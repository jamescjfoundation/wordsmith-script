# Pages and printing

Pages follow the screenplay convention: US Letter, Courier Prime 12 point, a
1.5 inch left margin, the page number top right with a period and none on
page 1. A speech that breaks across pages gets (MORE) and the name again
with (CONT'D); a speaker who speaks again after action in the same scene gets
(CONT'D) too. Scene CONTINUEDs can be switched on, and all of these texts
changed, with the **More and Continued Settings** commands in the command
palette. A scene heading is never
left alone at the foot of a page, and action and dialogue split only after a
sentence.

**Format › Title Page** edits the title page: title, credit, authors,
source, notes, contact, draft date and copyright. It prints before the
script, which is then numbered from 1.

**Format › Header and Footer** sets left, center and right text for each,
with `{page}`, `{scene}`, `{date}`, `{title}` and `{revision}` filled in on
every page, and whether page 1 shows them.

**File › Export PDF** (Mod+Shift+E) and **Print** (Mod+P) produce exactly
what the page view shows. Printing uses the system's print service (CUPS on
macOS and Linux; on Windows the PDF opens in your PDF viewer to print).
**Export Revised Pages** and **Print Revised Pages** send only pages with
revisions; see [Production](production.md).
