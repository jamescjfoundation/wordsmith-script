# Views

- **Page view** (Mod+Alt+2) shows the pages exactly as they print, with
  page numbers, (MORE) and (CONT'D), scene numbers and revision marks.
- **Normal view** (Mod+Alt+1) is the script in one continuous column.
- **Speed view** (Mod+Alt+3) puts every element at the left margin with its
  name beside it, for fast typing.
- **Index cards** (Mod+Alt+4): a card per scene, with its heading, summary
  (or the start of its action) and who speaks. Drag a card onto another to
  move the scene there; **Open** goes to it.
- **Scene view** (Mod+Alt+5): each scene as a block of its text; drag to
  reorder.
- **Story board** (Mod+Alt+6) and **Outline** (Mod+Alt+7): see
  [Outlining](outlining.md).
- **Tables** (Mod+Alt+T): a panel beside the script with three tables.
  Scenes: number, heading, page, length in eighths, who speaks, summary;
  click a heading or summary to edit it, drag a row to move the scene.
  Characters: speeches and scenes; edit a name to rename everywhere. Notes:
  every note, where it is attached. Scenes and characters export as CSV.

In the card, scene, board and outline views typing does not go into the
script; shortcuts still work, and **Escape** returns to the script.

**View** also has a **dark theme**, **typewriter scrolling** (the line you are
typing stays mid-window) and **focus mode** (Mod+Shift+F hides the menus and
bars; Escape brings them back). The view, theme, typewriter scrolling,
spelling and zoom are remembered between sessions.

The **command palette** (Mod+Shift+P) finds any command by name, including
the few that have no menu item yet.
