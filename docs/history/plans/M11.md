# M11 plan: characters and reports

Date: 2026-11-20

## Scope

SPEC-2 9.2, 10, 12 and 13: character traits; characters in a scene who do not
speak; highlighting a character's speeches; arc beats; a scene properties window
(title, color, summary, cast, arc beats); the scene, location, cast, character,
filtered-script, notes and statistics reports and the representation chart; table
sorting, filtering, column choice and the writer's own tabs; note titles, types,
colors and dates.

## Approach

- `scene_data.rs`: a scene's data keyed by an id on its heading paragraph
  (` scene=N` on the native `@` line), so it moves with the scene.
- `cast.rs`: traits, highlight color and switch, rename carries the data,
  `report.representation`.
- `reports_more.rs`: every report as sections of rows; `report.run`, `report.text`,
  `report.csv`; `report.export` and `report.exportFilteredPdf` in
  `wordsmith-format`.
- Notes gain title, type, color, created and modified (dates given by the caller,
  so the core needs no clock), `note.next/previous`, `note.types/setTypes`.
- `tables.tabs/setTabs` keep the writer's tabs with the script.
- Windows: Reports (Mod+Shift+J), Scene Properties, Character Details; the Tables
  panel rewritten column by column; speeches drawn tinted on screen only.
