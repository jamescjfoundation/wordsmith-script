# M7 plan: outlining

Date: 2026-11-19

## Scope

From the kickoff: a story board canvas of colored beats with title and body,
links between beats, and grid snap; send a beat to the script as outline
elements; an outline editor with lanes; show or hide outline elements in the
script.

Spec items (`docs/SPEC.md`, "Views, outlining, navigation and notes"):

| Spec item | Status |
| --- | --- |

Our name for the canvas is "Story board", as the spec suggests; the other
product's name for it is a trademark and is not used.

## Approach

The story board and the outline editor are two views of one set of beats,
kept in `app_data` (`wordsmith.storyboard`: beats, links, lanes, grid), so
both views, undo and saving share it, and every change is a command.

1. **`m7/board`.** Beats: id, title, body, color, position and size, and
   (for the outline) a lane and an order. Commands to add, change, move
   (snapping to the grid when it is on), delete (with its links), link and
   unlink beats, set the grid, and send a beat to the script: its title as
   an Outline 1 paragraph and its body as a Summary paragraph at the caret,
   the Outline 1 paragraph tagged with the beat (`ParaProps::beat`) so the
   outline can find it. The Story board view: a canvas with a dot grid,
   beats as colored cards, drag to move, double-click to add, a handle to
   draw links, a beat editor where Tab moves between title and body, a color
   palette, Send to Script.
2. **`m7/outline`.** Lanes: name and indent (0 to 2), in order; add, rename,
   move, indent, delete (its beats become unassigned). Beats sit in a lane in
   order; moving a beat between lanes or along one is a command. Templates
   make lanes for a structure (three acts; five acts; eight sequences;
   television: teaser, four acts and tag). The Outline view: one row per
   lane, its beats as cards in order, drag to reorder or change lane; the
   Script lane below shows the script's scenes, and beats sent to the script
   show the pages they cover.
3. **`m7/show-outline`.** A switch (`wordsmith.outline` `show`, on by
   default): off, outline elements (Outline 1 to 3, Summary, Note) leave the
   page view, the PDF and print, and the normal and speed views. The script
   keeps them, so turning it back on shows them where they were.

Decision to record: outlining (0028).

## Tests

- Beats: add, change, move with and without snap, delete (links go too),
  link both ways only once, hostile data bounded; send to script makes the
  two paragraphs at the caret, tagged.
- Lanes: add, rename, move, indent bounds, delete; beats across lanes in
  order; templates; page ranges of sent beats.
- Show and hide: hidden outline elements leave the PDF and the pages; the
  caret in a hidden paragraph is not lost; golden PDFs unchanged (no outline
  elements in them).
- Editor tests of the board and outline views (render, add, move, Tab).

Status: done (2026-11-19). As built: hiding outline elements applies to the
page view, PDF and print, not the normal and speed views (decision 0028).
