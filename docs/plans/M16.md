# M16 plan: side panels

Date: 2026-11-20. Part of `docs/plans/v0.3-ui.md`.

Deliverables: an icon rail at each side (`sidebar.rs`).

- **The Navigator:**
  - Scenes: filter, click to go, drag to move, right-click for properties, omit, recall, select.
  - Outline: a tree from structure ranks.
  - Bookmarks.
  - Notes: edit in place, filter.
  - Characters: highlight switch and color, traits, rename.
  - Find: results in context, replace.
- **The Inspector:**
  - Element: hint, formatting, change element.
  - Scene: title, color, summary, cast, arc beats.
  - Revisions.
  - Page: lock tools, page number, scene numbers.
  - Changes: accept or reject each.
  - Check Format.
  - Goals: today against goals, sprint.

Bookmarks, Scene Properties, Character Details and Check Format now open as panels.

Performance: the Scenes panel lays out only the rows in sight. The scene list reads the
scenes' data once instead of per scene. While typing goes on, the panel's list
refreshes at most every 300 ms; clicks always use the script as it is. Typing at
150 pages with the full chrome and the Navigator open: median 15.6 to 15.8 ms
(v0.2: 15.2 to 16.4 ms). `WS_TIMING_PARTS=1` times the frame without the panels
and without any chrome.

Tests: every panel draws and acts through commands; old windows' commands open
their panels; offscreen screenshot checked.

Status: done (2026-11-20). Panel widths and open panels are not yet kept in the
preferences (M19).
