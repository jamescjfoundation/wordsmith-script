# v0.2 plan: building out SPEC-2

Date: 2026-11-19

Milestones M9 to M14 take the items `docs/spec2-audit.md` marks as not built
or partly built. They follow SPEC-2's build order (section 20), starting
after what v0.1 already covers. Every milestone is additive (decision 0030).
Each one gets its own `docs/plans/M<n>.md` before work starts and waits for
the owner's go-ahead, as M1 to M8 did. Items depending on an open question in
`docs/spec2-questions.md` wait for its answer.

| Milestone | Scope | SPEC-2 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **M9 Editing** | Sequence element and structure rank; Enter/Tab hint in the status bar; reset to element; toggle case; auto-capitalise and smart quotes as options; abbreviations list; select scene; insert page break and new scene; go to page, bookmark and label; bookmarks and labels; find by whole word, direction and element; paste adds unknown elements; reformat tool; format checker | 3.1, 3.2, 10, 14 |
| **M10 Macros and formatting** | Macros (20 slots, aliases, active elements, copy from another script, a starter set on keys of our own); the remaining text styles; highlight palette; element settings for color, highlight, strikeout, lowercase, the indent rule and applying a template's table | 3.2, 5, 6 |
| **M11 Characters and reports** | Character traits; non-speaking characters; highlight speeches; arc beats; scene properties panel; location, cast, filtered-script, notes, statistics reports as new documents; table sorting, filtering, user tabs and columns; note fields and types | 10, 12, 13 |
| **M12 Planning** | Several story boards a script; align, distribute and cascade; link styles and colors; lists and emoji in beats; story board and card printing to PDF; a structure strip above the script with a page ruler and click-to-go; outline paragraphs drawn with nesting bars | 8, 9 |
| **M13 Production** | Dual dialogue; sides; remaining lock tools; scene-number options (prefix letters, restart, own font, editing one number); revision display and print choices; watermark; header and footer fields; print ranges and preview; change tracking; script comparison | 7, 11, 18 |
| **M14 Workspace and productivity** | Text document kind; templates and new-from-template; start window, recent files, reopen last; revert; split panels, zoom steps, document tabs, extra-dark theme; sprint timer, writing statistics, goals; editable shortcuts | 2, 8, 17, 19 |

Not scheduled until the owner decides (questions 9 and 10): breakdown
tagging, collaboration, the assistant, read-aloud, thesaurus and names lookup.
