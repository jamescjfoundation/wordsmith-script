## Approach

  television, stage, audio and prose, outlines); `wordsmith.kind` marks Text
  documents; `file.newFromTemplate`, `template.list`, `document.kind`.
- `wordsmith-format`: `file.saveAsTemplate` (`.wst`, opened as a new script),
  `file.revert`, `file.merge`; a read-only file opens as a copy with a notice.
- `wordsmith-ui::workspace`: tabs (the editor swaps sessions and forgets its caches),
  split panes (any structure view or the script beside or below), statistics kept in
  `stats.json` beside the preferences (words, pages, time by day and hour; streaks;
  best day and hours; bars with the goal line), sprints, the Welcome window, the
  template chooser, the Keys window.
- Preferences gain recent scripts, reopen last, the Welcome window, the writer's name,
  extra dark and keys.
