## Approach

- **Dual dialogue** (`paginate/dual.rs`, `dialogue.dual`, Mod+Shift+D): the
  first speech narrowed to the left half; the second drawn beside it as a text box
  and left out of the pages; a longer second speech pushes what follows down.
  Fountain's `^` reads and writes it.
- **Locks**: `lock.relock`, `lock.adjustBreak`, `lock.setLabel`, `lock.lockPage`,
  `lock.unlockPage`.
- **Scene numbers**: start, restart every N, prefix letters for added scenes,
  sides, own font; `scene.setNumber`; renumber from the caret or new scenes only.
- **Revisions**: a mark per set; only the current set showing; next and previous
  revised text.
- **Printing** (`PrintOptions`): pages or scenes, revised, unrevised or one set,
  reverse, title page, sides (a character's scenes with their numbers and the name
  on every page), collate, `file.printPreview`.
- **Watermark** (`watermark.rs`): diagonal text, strength, recipient.
- **Headers and footers**: `{collated}`, first page number, footer on page 1 apart
  from the header.
- **Change tracking** (`tracking.rs`): the document's tracked insertions and
  deletions, saved in the native format (`{sI3}`, `{sX3}` and the records).
- **Comparison** (`compare.rs`, `compare.withFile`): scenes matched by heading then
  text, paragraphs by longest common run, moved scenes noted; the result is the
  newer script with the differences as tracked changes.
