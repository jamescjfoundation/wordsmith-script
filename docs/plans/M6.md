# M6 plan: production

Date: 2026-11-19

## Scope

From the kickoff: revision mode with named, colored revision sets using the
standard color cycle, margin marks, mark and clear revised, and revision
colors in page view and PDF; scene numbering, shown or hidden; page locking
with A-pages, unlock one level, unlock all, OMITTED scenes and letter-suffixed
added scenes, with one documented page-label scheme; print or export revised
pages only; scene length in eighths of a page; a basic character report.

## Approach

Marks that must travel with the text are character marks, like notes
(decision 0023): `CharProps::revised` (the revision set) and
`CharProps::page_lock` (a locked page starts at this character). Scene numbers
and OMITTED are paragraph properties of the heading. Settings live in
`app_data`. The native format goes to version 3: `{r2}…{/r}` for revised text,
`{p7}` before a locked page's first character, and `#12A` / `omitted`
attributes on the heading's `@` line.

Margin marks and scene numbers are small text boxes in the output script,
anchored to their paragraph and placed in the margin (a spike showed they
land on the right line once offset by the paragraph's space before), so the
page view, the PDF and printing show the same thing. Page colors are a
rectangle behind the page.

1. **`m6/revisions`.** Settings (`wordsmith.revisions`): the sets (name,
   color, bold, underline, strikethrough), the current set, revision mode, the
   mark and its position, color whole pages in PDF. Commands: toggle mode,
   Next Revision (the next set in the cycle, made current, mode on), choose
   the current set, edit sets and options, mark revised, clear revised (the
   selection, or all), roll back to a set (clears the marks of later sets).
   In revision mode, typed text takes the current set; a deletion marks its
   paragraph (the mark shows on that paragraph's first line). The output puts
   a mark beside every revised line and applies the set's text styles; the
   page view tints each revised page's margins in the newest set's color.
2. **`m6/scene-numbers`.** Number scenes (1, 2, 3 …), show or hide numbers
   (left and right of the heading), lock scene numbers so new scenes take
   letters after the scene before them (12A, 12B; before scene 1, A1), omit a
   scene (the heading becomes OMITTED with its number; its text is kept in
   `app_data` and can be recalled), Fountain `#12A#` both ways.
3. **`m6/locking`.** Lock pages: mark each page's first character with a lock
   id and record its label and lock level. The planner starts a page at every
   lock mark (splitting a paragraph exactly there if needed) and never pulls
   text back across one; overflow pages take letters (decision: the
   page-label scheme). Relock adds a level; unlock one level removes the
   newest level's page starts; unlock all removes them all. Deleting the
   characters that start a page moves its mark to what follows; pages whose
   text is gone leave a placeholder page with a combined label (10-11).
   Locked pages show their labels in the header through one section per page.
4. **`m6/reports`.** Export and print revised pages only (and without marks),
   scene length in eighths of a page in the scenes table and CSV, and a
   character report (speeches, words, scenes, first and last scene, pages)
   as CSV.

Decisions to record: revisions (0024), scene numbers (0025), page locking and
the page-label scheme (0026), reports (in 0026 or its own).

## Tests

- Revisions: typing and deleting in and out of revision mode; mark and clear;
  next revision follows the cycle; rollback; marks on the right lines in the
  PDF (text extraction); page colors; native round trip; migration from 2.
- Scene numbers: numbering, locked letters, omit and recall with undo,
  show and hide in the PDF, Fountain both ways.
- Locking: locked labels survive edits; overflow letters; placeholders;
  unlock one level and all; native round trip; the PDF header labels.
- Reports: revised-only pages, eighths arithmetic, the character report.
- The definition of done's scenario end to end: draft, lock, Blue revision,
  print revised pages only.
