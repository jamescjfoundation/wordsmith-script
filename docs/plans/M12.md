# M12 plan: planning

Date: 2026-11-20

## Scope

SPEC-2 8 and 9: several story boards per script; arranging beats on the grid, in a
column, in a row or as a cascade; link styles (line, forward, back, both) and
colors; simple lists in beats; the story board and index cards printed to PDF
(3 × 5 card stock or a grid, frames, grey, chosen cards); cards per row and
cards showing script text or summaries; the structure strip above the script; outline
paragraphs drawn with nesting bars.

## Approach

- `storyboard.rs`: boards (`storyboard.addBoard/renameBoard/deleteBoard`), a board
  per beat, `beats.arrange`, link `style` and `color`, `beat.styleLink`. Older
  boards read as one board with forward links.
- `planning_pdf.rs`: the board and the cards built as documents of shapes and
  printed with the script's layout and PDF writer; `storyboard.exportPdf` and
  `cards.exportPdf` in `wordsmith-format`.
- `strip.rs`: lanes of blocks by page span from the structure ranks (decision
  0031); `strip.get/configure`.
- Interface: board tabs, Shift-click selection, Arrange and Links menus, list
  buttons; card options and Print Cards; a strip panel with its own zoom
  (Mod+Alt+S); nesting bars in the page view.

Decision to record: 0034.

## Tests

Boards keep their beats and links and the last board stays; arrange in every way;
old boards read; the board PDF holds beats, lines and arrowheads inside the page;
cards fill grids or card stock; strip lanes follow acts, sequences and scenes and
are configurable; list lines number on; a headless window test of boards, strip and
cards.

Status: done (2026-11-20). Emoji in beats show only where the interface fonts
have them (no emoji font is bundled; `docs/gaps.md`).
